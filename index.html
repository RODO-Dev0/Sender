Converts an image into an acoustic spectrogram and plays it through your speakers. A receiver listening nearby can rebuild the picture from the sound alone — no file transfer, no network.

How it works

  1. Open receiver.html on the listening device.
  2. Press Start Listening there first.
  3. Come back here and press Play. Keep the two devices a few feet apart, in a quiet room if possible.

Testing alone? Just let this device's speaker play into its own microphone — open both pages in separate tabs or windows.

Microphone access needs a secure context (HTTPS or localhost). Opening the receiver as a local file:// page may block mic access in some browsers — GitHub Pages works reliably.
